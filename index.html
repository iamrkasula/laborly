Time contractions
with one tap.

A calm contraction timer and kick counter for expecting parents and their birth partners, on iPhone and Apple Watch.

One big button

Start and Stop. The time keeps running when you lock your iPhone or close the app. Length, time apart and the last hour, worked out for you.

From the Lock Screen

A Live Activity and the Dynamic Island show the running time, with Start and Stop that work without unlocking. Widgets, Control Center and Siri too.

On your wrist

An Apple Watch app with a complication. It keeps timing when your iPhone isn't nearby and catches up when it's back.

Your care team's pattern

Pick 5-1-1, 4-1-1, 3-1-1, 3 in 10 or your own. Laborly tells you calmly when your contractions match it, never during a contraction.

History and reports

Sessions with charts. Edit any contraction, add one you missed, log labor events, and share a PDF report with your care team.

Kicks, guides, bag

A kick counter, short guides with their sources (WHO, ACOG, NHS) and a hospital bag checklist you can make your own.

Private

No account and no analytics. Your data stays on your iPhone and, with iCloud Sync on, in your own private iCloud.

Updating from Contraction Timer: Counter?
